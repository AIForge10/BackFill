BACKFILL / FDA SHORTAGE RESEARCH

Our hypothesis

When a drug enters shortage, manufacturers that can still supply it may capture demand from disrupted competitors. We test buying confirmed available suppliers after a 20-session wait, holding five sessions, and shorting SPY in proportion to each stock's prior beta to reduce broad market exposure.
